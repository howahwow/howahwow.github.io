Harbour Hostel

A working building near the harbour, Soviet-era in construction and now operating as a hostel — the kind of structure that never gets a heritage listing and never gets a photographer either.

Most of what was built in this period was not a palace or a memorial. It was this: ordinary fabric, put up quickly, still standing because there has been no particular reason to take it down. Recording it is the only way the picture of the period stays honest.

Photographs and text by Dicky Chan · shot on iPhone, on foot
